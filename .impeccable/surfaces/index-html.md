## Scope
App shell and all working screens of Depósito común (Resumen, Viajes, Repostajes, Gastos, Perfil, formularios). Mode: Operate. Mobile-first (used at the car and the pump), desktop secondary.

## Audience and task
Five family members sharing a 2005 C3. Log trip km with odometer evidence in seconds; log refuels with receipt; see who owes whom and mark Bizum sent/received; review own totals by date, tramo and trip.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The fuel-station price totem: figures that matter (what you owe, €/l, odometer) are lit in a dark panel as segment digits, row label left, number right. Refuses the white-card fintech dashboard with a navy accent and donut charts.
OWN-WORLD: Graphite totem panels and rail (near-black, slightly warm-blue) on a pale pylon-grey ground; one amber LED accent for lit figures and the primary action; green paid, red owed/missing only where they mean that. Seven-segment SVG digits with faint unlit segments; Archivo for UI with tabular numerals. Square-ish 6px corners, hairline dividers, dense tables.
STORY: Open, read your balance on the totem, see any evidence warning, log a trip or a refuel, mark your Bizum. Every figure traces to a photo or is flagged.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Mobile: top bar with brand + avatar; totem panel with 3–4 lit rows (Debes / Te deben / Cuentakm. / Último €/l); below it the evidence alerts strip, then two primary actions (Registrar viaje, Repostar); bottom tab bar. Desktop: dark left rail nav, totem panel spanning the top of the work area, recent trips table and pending payments side by side.
FORM: Pick card (grounded candidate 1, price totem), not the assigned index 4. seed:424303efa908a630
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
